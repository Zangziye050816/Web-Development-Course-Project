As we all know, colorful and beautiful Paper Cuttings is the unique style of Yuxian County. People who have visited the production process of Yuxian Paper Cuttings have a common feeling, that is, Yuxian Paper Cuttings is bold and pungent in color design, never restricted by the framework of solid color, light source color, etc., and can do whatever they want, but it is different from random graffiti without rules. Why is this? Because Yuxian Paper Cuttings has its unique way of expression in color, it seems unorganized for laymen, but it is also strictly followed in practice. To put it in one sentence, it is: its understanding of color is rational, the form of expressing color is symbolic, and the application of color is subjective. Its color suddenly appears bright red and green, incredibly dazzling; Carefully savoring, I feel that the combination is very fitting and harmonious, full of wit and humor, without any astringency or stiffness. It is this unique expression of color that creates the bright and gorgeous style of Yuxian Paper Cuttings and gives people a joyful and joyful artistic enjoyment visually
