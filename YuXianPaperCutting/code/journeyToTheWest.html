Journey To
The West
Chinese Traditional novel

| Introduction |

The novel mainly tells the story of Sun Wukong, who was born, followed Bodhi Patriarch to learn art and caused chaos in the Heavenly Palace. He met Tang Monk, Zhu Bajie, Sha Monk, and White Dragon Horse, traveled westward to obtain Buddhist scriptures, went through hardships,conquered demons and monsters, experienced 981 difficulties, and finally arrived in the Western Heaven to meet the Buddha, and finally the Five Saints came true. The novel is based on the historical event of Xuanzang's pilgrimage, and through the author's artistic processing, it deeply depicts the social and living conditions during the Ming Dynasty.
