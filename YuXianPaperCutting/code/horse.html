In traditional Chinese culture, the horse is one of the twelve zodiac animals and ranks seventh, corresponding to the "noon" in the earthly branch.
Horses are regarded as representatives of vitality, with the symbolism of divine power! In addition, horses also have the meaning of "leading the way" and "galloping horizontally", and are regarded as symbols of fame and career advancement!

"Pony Pony" was carved and signed by Mr. Zhou Yongsheng, the representative inheritor of the third generation of Yuxian Paper Cuttings intangible cultural heritage.
