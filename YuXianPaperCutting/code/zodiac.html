| Introduction |

The Zodiac, also known as the Chinese Horoscope, is a unique and fascinating aspect of traditional Chinese culture that has been passed down through generations. It represents a cyclical time reckoning system based on twelve animal signs, each associated with particular personality traits, compatibilities, and destinies.
