Have you ever seen Paper Cuttings without scissors

Smoked samples

Place the carved Paper Cuttings sample on white paper or yellow paper with fur edges, wet the paper surface with towel, place it on a small board for rubbing, smoke it with oil lamp or candle smoke, and print the shape of the sample completely and clearly on the paper surface as the sample for engraving. In modern times, sun exposure is often used instead of fumigation.

Customized compression

According to the specifications of the pattern, place the smoked sample on a rice paper board made of dozens of rice paper sheets, fix it with paper twists and nails, wet the cardboard with water, and then cover it with heavy objects to fully compact the paper board. Squeeze out the moisture, hang and dry it, and it will become flat and hard pieces of paper board

Engraving

Use self-made carving knives of various styles for manual carving, requiring consistency in top and bottom, non stick and non connected, and clean and neat. When carving, it is necessary to first carve the fine parts and then the coarse parts, and first carve the middle part and then the two sides. The combination of Yin and Yang carving needs to be clever

Dye

The product color shall be mixed with alcohol or Baijiu, which shall be blended into various colors according to the needs of color samples, and the works shall be completed by flat painting, halo dyeing, spot dyeing, overprint dyeing, rendering, washing dyeing, supplement dyeing and other methods with a brush
