Yu County, located in the northwest of Hebei Province, is famous for producing Paper Cuttings< br> Yuxian Paper Cuttings, also known as "window cut", is a traditional decorative art among the local people. It has a history of more than 200 years. The initial patterns are mostly auspicious patterns like flowers. Later, they were integrated into the artistic characteristics of Tianjin willow youth paintings and Wuqiang New Year paintings, forming their own unique style. Through the joint research of Liu Laobu and other Paper Cuttings artists, the creation tools were reformed, and some patterns were carved in batches instead of single cutting, which improved the Paper Cuttings technology in Yuxian. At the beginning of the 20th century, driven by Wang Laoshang, Wang Shouye, Zhou Yongming and other artists, the style of Paper Cuttings in Yuxian County became more and more prominent, went out of the general pattern of folk Paper Cuttings, and had a deeper humanistic connotation

Fingertip flowers Paper Cuttings in Yu County, Hebei

|Yuxian Paper Cuttings originally originated from rock murals. After countless silent years, it has finally become a modern "official". Modern Yuxian Paper Cuttings was founded in the period from Daoguang to Guangxu in the Qing Dynasty, and has been nearly 200 years of history. Paper Cuttings is generally called "window flower" in the local area. Tianpi Liang "is the earliest form of window decoration, which involves painting and coloring on mica sheets for decoration< br> |In the early days, it was still popular in the local area to provide flower shoes and embroidered "patterns" on handbags and pillows. Later, Wuqiang woodblock watermark window cut and willow youth painting were introduced. Peasant artists learned from the Paper Cuttings form of "pattern", absorbed the color characteristics of Wuqiang woodblock watermark window cut and willow youth painting, and imitated the transparent effect of "bright sky". After hundreds of years, they constantly explored and excavated the techniques, and finally formed the "spot dyed Paper Cuttings with engraving instead of cutting, intaglio as the main, and sun engraving as the auxiliary" technique
